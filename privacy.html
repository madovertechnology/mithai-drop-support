Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Mithai Drop: Merge Puzzle is made by Mad Over Technology. Contact us at hello@madovertech.io about privacy questions or data requests.

Data stored on your device

On first launch and when changing players, the game asks for a date of birth to show appropriate features. The date is used on that screen and is not saved or sent to us. Only an “under 18” or “adult” choice is saved on your device. The game also saves your best score, sound and music settings, an adult analytics choice, purchase status, and adult scores waiting to be submitted to a gaming leaderboard. Core gameplay works offline. Deleting the app removes local game data, subject to your device backup settings.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics for Firebase is unavailable to players under 18. For adults, it is off until they enable “Share game analytics” in Settings. When enabled, Google processes gameplay events such as rounds, scores, session duration, sweet tiers, and leaderboard opens, along with app-instance identifiers and device/app information. We do not put names, email addresses, or gaming-account IDs in custom analytics events. Turning analytics off stops future collection and resets local analytics data; it does not delete data Google previously received. Contact us for data requests.

Advertising

The game can show Google AdMob banner ads below the game board. Full-screen ads between rounds are available only to adults. Ad requests for players under 18 are marked child-directed and limited to G-rated content and Families-certified ad sources. The Android advertising ID permission is blocked. All requests are non-personalized and use Google's consent flow where required. Ad delivery can still process IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. “Ad privacy choices” in Settings opens Google's privacy options for adults when available; younger players' ad settings are automatic. See Google's privacy policy.

Optional purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase available only in the adult experience and handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store processes payment information and returns purchase status so the game can hide ads and restore the purchase. Ad-free status is also kept locally so a younger player on the same device does not see ads after an adult buys it. We do not receive your payment-card details or operate a separate purchase-account database.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards and board sharing are available only in the adult experience. Game Center on iPhone and Play Games on Android are optional. When an adult signs in, scores can be submitted to that platform and shown according to its profile settings. The game keeps pending scores on the device for retry. Apple and Google manage the gaming accounts and their deletion controls; we do not operate a separate player-account database.

Device access

Mithai Drop does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location. It uses audio output and optional haptic feedback for game effects.

Ad privacy choices and your rights

The game requests non-personalized ads only, and we do not sell your personal information. Where privacy law requires it, Google's consent tool gives adult players a choice:

  • EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, you are asked whether Google and its advertising partners may store and access information on your device for advertising. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time with “Ad privacy choices” in Settings.
  • US states with privacy laws: where Google offers it in your state, “Ad privacy choices” in Settings lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising.

For players under 18, ad requests are always child-directed and marked as under the age of consent, so these choices are managed automatically.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or under US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Game data stays on your device and we hold no copy of it. Data processed for ads and optional analytics is held by Google: use the choices above, turn analytics off in Settings, or use Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. Leaderboard scores are held by Apple or Google under your gaming account. You can also email us with any request, and if you are in the EEA or UK you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Choices and contact

Adults can turn analytics on or off in Settings and use ad privacy options where available. Anyone can turn music and sound on or off, change the player age choice, and play without a gaming account. Email hello@madovertech.io with privacy questions.
